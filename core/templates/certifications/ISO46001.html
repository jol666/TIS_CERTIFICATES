Understanding the standard

What is ISO 46001?

ISO 46001 sets out requirements for a water efficiency management system, with guidance for putting it into practice.

It applies to organizations that use water, across different sizes and sectors. Its approach considers reducing consumption, replacing water sources where suitable and reusing water where feasible.

Purpose and performance

Why it matters

A practical approach starts with understanding your organization and setting clear priorities.

  • Resource efficiency. Identify avoidable consumption and opportunities to reduce waste.
  • Clearer accountability. Connect water performance with responsibilities and measurable targets.
  • Evidence of progress. Use records and reviews to demonstrate how your system is improving.

Before you begin

Common questions

Is ISO 46001 only for water utilities?

No. Organizations in manufacturing, services and other sectors can apply the standard when they use water.

Can it work alongside other management systems?

It can be integrated with systems such as ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 50001.

How do we maintain performance?

Monitor water use, review objectives and act on findings to keep improving.
