A foundation for quality

What is GMP?

Good Manufacturing Practices establish the conditions and working methods that support consistent production and product quality.

They address the manufacturing environment, equipment, personnel, records and operational controls. The applicable requirements depend on your products, industry and destination market.

For food businesses, sound manufacturing and hygiene practices provide a foundation for systems such as HACCP and ISO 22000. TIS Certification is based in Kuwait; contact our team to discuss your site, products and assessment needs.

From premises to production

What does a GMP assessment cover?

Premises & equipment

Suitable layouts, maintained equipment and cleaning arrangements that help protect products from contamination.

People & hygiene

Clear responsibilities, appropriate protective clothing, personnel hygiene and training relevant to each role.

Production & records

Documented working methods, material identification and records that make production activities traceable.

Review & corrective action

Processes for investigating problems, addressing their causes and checking that corrective actions work.

Better control, greater confidence

Why focus on GMP?

Strong everyday practices help your team maintain standards as production demands change.

  • More consistent operations. Give teams clear procedures for the work they perform.
  • Stronger hygiene controls. Address contamination risks through the design and upkeep of your workplace.
  • Clearer evidence. Keep organized records for internal review and customer assessments.
  • Continuous improvement. Use findings to prioritize action and review progress.

Before you begin

Common questions

Which businesses can use GMP?

GMP is relevant to manufacturing sectors including food, pharmaceuticals and medical devices. Each sector has different requirements; discuss your products and market before selecting a scheme.

Is GMP the same as ISO 22000?

No. GMP focuses on manufacturing practices and conditions. ISO 22000 addresses a food safety management system. They can support complementary objectives.

Does certification replace regulatory approval?

A third-party assessment does not replace any required licence or regulatory inspection. The scope and recognition of an assessment should be confirmed for your intended market.

How long does an assessment take?

Timing depends on site size, scope, readiness and any corrective actions. Contact TIS with your requirements to discuss the next steps.
