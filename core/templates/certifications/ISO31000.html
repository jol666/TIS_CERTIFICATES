Understanding the standard

What is ISO 31000?

ISO 31000 offers guidance for managing risk through a set of principles, an organizational framework and a practical process.

Organizations of any size can use it to connect risk management with their objectives. It is guidance, rather than a certifiable management system standard.

Why it matters

A practical approach starts with understanding your organization and setting clear priorities.

  • Clear priorities. Focus attention on the uncertainties that matter to your objectives.
  • Consistent decisions. Give teams a shared approach to discussing risk.
  • Better visibility. Make risk information available to the people who need it.

Before you begin

Common questions

Can an organization be certified to ISO 31000?

ISO 31000 is not intended for organizational certification. It can be used as a benchmark for reviewing risk management practices.

Who can use it?

The guidance can be applied across industries and organization sizes.
